# Tilted cylinder falls over by itself in Gazebo Harmonic with Bullet-featherstone

Note, 2026-11-28. Not filed upstream. Simulation only.

## Summary
A rigid cylinder (radius 25 mm, length 80 mm, mass 0.1 kg) resting on a flat static box and started with a small tilt should rock and settle upright for any tilt below atan(25/40) = 32.0 deg. With the Bullet-featherstone engine the rocking often does not die out: the cylinder keeps wobbling, or falls over. This happens with no robot in the scene. The same scene under DART behaves as expected.

## Versions
Ubuntu 22.04.5, gz-harmonic 1.0.0, libgz-sim8 8.15.0, libgz-physics7 7.8.0, libbullet3.06 3.06, libdart6.12 6.12.1, libsdformat14 14.9.0.

## Reproduction (standalone, no ROS)
`testing/_out/rock_sim.py <variant> [sim_seconds]` builds a world with one cylinder per case, each on its own 0.5 x 0.5 x 0.04 m static box (top at z = 1.055), steps it with the Python TestFixture and reads the poses after 12 s of simulation time. `TILTS=20,24,29` overrides the starting tilts. Results are appended to `testing/_out/rock_sim.csv`. Both files are local and git-ignored.

The essential scene:

```xml
<physics name="p" type="bullet"><max_step_size>0.001</max_step_size></physics>
<plugin filename="gz-sim-physics-system" name="gz::sim::systems::Physics">
  <engine><filename>gz-physics-bullet-featherstone-plugin</filename></engine>
</plugin>
<model name="table"><static>true</static><pose>0 0 1.035 0 0 0</pose><link name="link">
  <collision name="c"><geometry><box><size>0.5 0.5 0.04</size></box></geometry></collision></link></model>
<!-- 5 deg tilt: z = 1.055 + 0.04 cos(a) + 0.025 sin(a) + 0.0003 -->
<model name="cylinder"><pose>0 0 1.09733 0.0872665 0 0</pose><link name="link">
  <inertial><mass>0.1</mass><inertia><ixx>2e-4</ixx><iyy>2e-4</iyy><izz>2e-4</izz>
    <ixy>0</ixy><ixz>0</ixz><iyz>0</iyz></inertia></inertial>
  <collision name="collision">
    <geometry><cylinder><radius>0.025</radius><length>0.08</length></cylinder></geometry>
    <surface><friction><ode><mu>1.5</mu><mu2>1.5</mu2></ode></friction></surface>
  </collision></link></model>
```

Each cell below uses 10 tilt directions, 36 deg apart. "Toppled" means final tilt above 45 deg or centre more than 10 mm below the resting height (this includes cylinders that fell off their box).

## Engine and time step, cylinder primitive (toppled out of 10)
| Engine, step | 0 deg | 2 deg | 5 deg | 15 deg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bullet-featherstone, 1 ms | 0 | 4 | 6 | 2 |
| Bullet-featherstone, 0.5 ms | 0 | 7 | 6 | 2 |
| Bullet-featherstone, 0.25 ms | 1 | 9 | 6 | 3 |
| Bullet (non-featherstone plugin), 1 ms | 0 | 5 | 4 | 1 |
| DART, 1 ms | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Cylinders counted as not toppled under either Bullet plugin were often still tilted after 12 s (up to 27 deg with featherstone, 39 deg with the other plugin). Under DART all of them ended at 0.00 deg and the correct height. A smaller time step did not help.

## Collision shape (toppled out of 10, 1 ms)
Meshes are closed prisms generated by the script.

| Collision shape | 0 / 2 / 5 / 15 deg | Resting centre height (correct 1.0950) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 64, 32 or 16-sided mesh, full size, featherstone | 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 | 1.1050 | floats 10 mm high |
| 64-sided mesh shrunk by 10 mm on every side, featherstone | 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 | 1.0950 | candidate fix |
| 64-sided mesh, `optimization="convex_hull"`, featherstone | 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 | 1.0965 | at 5 and 15 deg some stayed tilted about 8 deg |
| same, shrunk by 10 mm | 0 / 10 / 8 / 4 | 1.0864 at 0 deg | unusable |
| 64-sided mesh, DART | 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 | 1.0950 | |

## Where each one tips (toppled out of 10, 1 ms)
| Case | 20 deg | 24 deg | 29 deg | 35 deg | 45 deg | 90 deg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DART, primitive | 0 | 0 | 0 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Featherstone, full-size mesh | 0 | 0 | 0 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Featherstone, shrunk mesh | 0 | 0 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 |

The shrunk mesh lies on its side with its centre 25 mm above the table, so its effective radius is correct. The 10 mm offset behaves like a collision margin added around the mesh, which would round the rim with a 10 mm radius; a rim like that is expected to tip at atan(15/30) = 26.6 deg, consistent with falling between 24 and 29 deg. The margin explanation is inferred from the behaviour and from the plugin using a Bullet GImpact mesh shape; the plugin source was not read.

## Same effect in the full robot world
`testing/_out/tilt_test.py` (UR3 with Robotiq 2F-140 present but idle, `colored_cylinder.world`, 12 s wall-clock wait), toppled out of 10:

| Inertia | 2 deg | 5 deg | 10 deg | 15 deg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| World file (2e-4 on all axes) | 0 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Solid cylinder (6.9e-5, 6.9e-5, 3.1e-5) | 0 | 10 | 10 | 10 |

A no-tilt drop test in the same world (0, 1, 5 and 15 mm drops, 20 each) toppled 0 of 80.

## Full pick-and-place with the shrunk mesh
UR3 with Robotiq 2F-140, MoveIt Task Constructor, place point y 0.20, object pose read 12 s after the result, success = within 20 mm. Run 2026-11-28 in four fresh simulations of 5 trials (`testing/_out/mesh_bench/`).

| Cylinder collision | Trials | Within 20 mm | Toppled | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primitive (current) | 20 | 4 | 14 | 1 pick failure |
| Shrunk 64-sided mesh | 20 | 18 | 0 | 2 arm stalls, object never moved |

With the mesh, the 18 executed trials had a median error of 6.3 mm (maximum 19.0 mm) and a median cycle of 27.1 s. The two stalls were consecutive trials in one simulation, both with wrist_1 1.92 rad from the planned end pose. The primitive-cylinder runs were made earlier the same day, not interleaved with these, at different machine load.

## Limits of the evidence
- The two setups disagree in detail: the full world toppled 100% at 5 deg and above and 0% at 2 deg; the standalone scene toppled a fraction at 2, 5 and 15 deg. The cause of the difference was not studied.
- One cylinder size, one mass, one friction value, one table, one machine, one set of package versions. The simulation is deterministic enough that the 10 cases differ only by tilt direction and position, so the counts are not statistical rates.
- The mechanism inside the engine is unknown. "Adds energy" is read from the motion, not measured as energy.
- The standalone scene sets friction only under `<ode>`, as the project worlds do; whether Bullet-featherstone reads it was not checked.
- DART is not a drop-in fix for this project: an earlier trial (2026-09-19, older packages) broke controller activation.
- The shrunk mesh's rim differs from a real sharp-edged cylinder.
- A web search found no documented report of this behaviour; the search was brief and is not proof that none exists.
